# Notion sign-in setup

The active app uses the Notion social provider in Supabase Auth. Configure the Notion integration callback in Supabase, then follow [SUPABASE_SETUP.md](SUPABASE_SETUP.md) for the provider, redirects, migrations, and environment variables.

The former direct Express OAuth callback documented in earlier revisions of this file is no longer registered. Its history remains available in Git.
